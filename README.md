# Cloud Kitchen Data Analytics

End-to-end data analytics project on a cloud kitchen business using Excel, SQL, and Power BI — covering data cleaning, analysis, and dashboard reporting to uncover business insights around customers, menu performance, and orders.

## 📊 Key Insights

- Zomato is the top revenue channel, contributing ₹4.43L (51%) of total sales, followed by Swiggy and Direct orders.
- Starters is the best-performing category, generating over ₹5.4L in sales and ₹1.6L in profit — more than half of total revenue.
- Total revenue for FY 2024-25 was ₹8.68L across 1,844 orders, with a 7.7% cancellation rate.
- Evening hours see the highest order volume, consistent with the snack-focused menu.

## 📌 About

This project analyzes one year of real operational data from a cloud kitchen business I ran, covering customer behavior, menu performance, and order patterns to support data-driven business decisions.
